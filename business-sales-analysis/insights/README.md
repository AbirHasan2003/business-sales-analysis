# Business Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes business sales data using MySQL to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, payment methods, and sales trends over time.

## Dataset Summary

* Total records: **500**
* Total sales: **155,680.00**
* Total cost: **109,193.00**
* Total profit: **46,867.00**
* Overall profit margin: **30.09%**

## Business Recommendations

1. **Monitor Electronics margins**
   Electronics generates substantial revenue and profit, but its margin is lower than Clothing and Stationery. Product-level pricing and cost analysis could identify opportunities to improve profitability.

2. **Investigate high-margin products**
   Products such as Pen, Mouse, Shirt, T-Shirt, and Notebook have relatively high profit margins. Their sales volume and inventory availability should be monitored.

3. **Analyze regional profitability**
   Dhaka produces the highest sales, while some other regions show higher profit margins. Management can investigate differences in pricing, product mix, and operating costs.

4. **Maintain strong digital payment support**
   Card and Mobile Banking together represent a large portion of recorded sales. Maintaining reliable digital payment options can support customer convenience.

5. **Focus on customer profitability, not only revenue**
   Customers with high sales do not necessarily have the highest profit margins. Customer-level analysis should consider both revenue and profitability.

6. **Track monthly fluctuations**
   Monthly sales vary significantly. Monitoring seasonal patterns can help with inventory planning, promotions, and sales forecasting.
